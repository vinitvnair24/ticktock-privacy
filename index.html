Privacy Policy — Clock Learning

Android app com.vinit.clocklearning · Published by Vivian24 · Effective 22 October 2026

Clock Learning is a teaching clock for children aged 3 to 8. We built it to collect nothing about the children who use it, or about their parents.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect names, email addresses, location, photos, contacts or device identifiers. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

The microphone

The "Say the time" game asks for the microphone so a child can say the time out loud. Speech is turned into text on the device itself using Android's on-device speech recogniser. The audio is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and the text is used only to check the answer and is then discarded. If on-device speech is not available, the game offers answers to tap instead. Parents can turn the microphone permission off at any time in Android Settings.

What is stored on the device

The app keeps two small settings on the tablet or phone: how many free games have been played, and whether the one-time unlock has been bought. They never leave the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We never see card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the unlock has been bought on this Google account, so it can open the games. A grown-up check (a simple maths question) appears before any purchase.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families policy. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to review, export or delete. The app contains no ads and no links to other apps or websites inside the child's area.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
